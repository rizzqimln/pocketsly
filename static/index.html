Get Pocketsly on your phone

Free, private, and works offline. Anyone can install it on their own device in under a minute — no app store needed.

Android

  1. Open Pocketsly in Chrome
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen
  3. Done — it opens like an app

iPhone & iPad

  1. Open Pocketsly in Safari
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen
  3. Done — it works offline too

Computer

  1. Open Pocketsly in Chrome or Edge
  2. Click the install icon in the address bar
  3. Done — launches like a real app

On iPhone or iPad: use Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.
